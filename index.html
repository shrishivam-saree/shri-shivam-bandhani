About Us

Celebrate Culture with Every Drape

Shri Shivam is a saree boutique inspired by the rich tradition of Rajasthani craftsmanship. We bring you handcrafted Bandhani sarees made with vibrant colors, intricate tie-dye patterns, and graceful artistry that suits weddings, family functions, and festive moments.

Our collection is curated to offer elegance, comfort, and authenticity, helping every woman feel proud, graceful, and beautifully dressed in the spirit of Rajasthan.
